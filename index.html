About

I'm Muzi, a software developer based in Standerton, Mpumalanga, with about six years of experience building for the web.

Through Abas Systems I build booking software for small businesses: salons, spas, barbers and clinics. Your clients book online, pay a deposit and get reminders, so you spend less time on the phone and lose fewer appointments.

I also build websites and custom web apps for businesses that want to work online.

  • My stack:
    HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, MySQL, MongoDB, Bootstrap, Tailwind, Git and GitHub, Figma.
